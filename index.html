I am a Ph.D. student at the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington, advised by Prof. Mari Ostendorf, and closely collaborating with Prof. Noah A. Smith. I build speech-based multimodal AI systems for conversation: voice agents that listen in real time, audio language models that treat every speaker fairly, and speech recognition that works beyond high-resource languages.

My recent work studies stateful inference for low-latency streaming ASR in voice agents and accent bias in audio language models; the latter received an Outstanding Paper Award at EMNLP 2026. Before UW, I earned a B.Tech. + M.Tech. dual degree in Electrical Engineering (Minor in AI & Data Science) from IIT Bombay, graduating as the department valedictorian. There I worked on multilingual ASR with Prof. Preethi Jyothi and on on-device inference with Prof. Sharayu Moharir. I have also spent time at UT Austin, Texas Instruments, Kyushu Institute of Technology, and Siemens.
